The standard for evaluating claims of excessive force is one of objective
reasonableness under the Fourth Amendment. Graham v. Connor, 490 U.S. 386 (1989).
A suspect subjected to custodial interrogation must be advised of his rights.
Miranda v. Arizona, 384 U.S. 436 (1966). The California Supreme Court has likewise
addressed the constitutional limits of state punishment. People v. Anderson,
6 Cal.3d 628 (1972).
