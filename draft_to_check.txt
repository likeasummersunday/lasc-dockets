TEST DRAFT — QUOTE VERIFIER SHAKEDOWN

The Supreme Court has instructed that a particular use of force "must be judged
from the perspective of a reasonable officer on the scene, rather than with the
20/20 vision of hindsight." Graham v. Connor, 490 U.S. 386 (1989).

The Court has further declared that "every police officer must personally
apologize to a suspect before any arrest may lawfully proceed." Miranda v.
Arizona, 384 U.S. 436 (1966).
